Learn full-stack development using my WhatsApp bot as a project.

## Resources
- The Odin Project (Foundations, JavaScript, Node)
- Full Stack Open (Parts 0-3, then 4-7)

## Weekly Rhythm
- 30–60 min/day: one small lesson
- 1–2 times/week: apply to bot
- Don't rewrite everything. Change one thing, test, commit.
